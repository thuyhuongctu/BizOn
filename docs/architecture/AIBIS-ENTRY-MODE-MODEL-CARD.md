# AIBIS Entry Mode Engine — Model Card

Version: 0.1.0  
Status: evidence-informed simulation model; not empirically calibrated for real investment advice.

## Purpose
Rank six entry modes for an educational simulation: Export, Licensing, Joint Venture, Strategic Alliance, Wholly Owned FDI, and Digital Entry.

## Scientific logic
1. **Transaction cost economics:** entry modes differ in control and resource commitment; efficiency depends on firm, product, and environmental conditions.
2. **Resource-based view:** firm capabilities and knowledge protection needs condition feasible modes.
3. **Institutional perspective:** formal and informal distance affect governance costs, but the direction is heterogeneous and context-dependent.
4. **Digital internationalization:** digital/platform entry changes the role of physical commitment, network effects, ecosystem dependence, data regulation, and local embeddedness.
5. **Meta-analytic caution:** no mode is universally superior. Antecedent–mode and mode–outcome relationships vary across context and measurement.

## Inputs
- Strategic priorities: control, speed, learning, capital efficiency, risk compatibility, knowledge protection, local embeddedness, digital scalability.
- Firm readiness: financial capacity, international experience, digital capability.
- Country profile: institutional and cultural distance, tariff pressure, logistics, IP protection, partner value, political risk, digital readiness, network effects, data-regulation risk.

## Scoring
Final score = 55% weighted strategic fit + 20% firm-capability fit + 20% country–mode fit + 5% distance adjustment.

This weighting is a transparent simulation design choice, not an estimated causal coefficient. It must be calibrated through expert review, sensitivity analysis, pilot data, and later empirical validation.

## Guardrails
- Do not label the highest score as the objectively correct entry mode.
- Display score components, confidence, strengths, cautions, and assumptions.
- Do not use for financial or legal advice.
- Keep digital entry confidence lower until evidence is stronger and more consistent.
- Preserve deterministic outputs for the same registry, context, and engine version.

## Validation plan
1. Content validity with 5–10 international-business experts.
2. Sensitivity analysis across all weight combinations and country archetypes.
3. Known-groups tests using scripted firm profiles.
4. Convergent review against expert rankings.
5. Classroom pilot for decision quality and learning outcomes.
6. Versioned calibration registry; never silently change coefficients.
