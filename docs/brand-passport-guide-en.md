# Brand Passport: Guide for International Classes

**For:** instructors running a class session or pilot session, and students playing on their own or in class.

**Play now:** https://thuyhuongctu.github.io/BizOn/brand-passport.html
**Language:** the game is fully bilingual. Click the "⋯" menu icon (top left) and switch to English at any time, mid-game included, without losing progress or re-rolling any random outcome.
**Guide videos:** a short version and a detailed version, both with English captions, embedded at the top of the play page (buttons 🎬 Short / 📖 Detailed inside the game) and on the intro page `gioi-thieu.html`.

## 1 · Overview

Brand Passport is a strategy simulation about **internationalizing a business**, played over **6 business quarters**. Each player takes over one of 4 Vietnamese companies and expands the brand into 6 fictional markets under conditions of **"information fog"**: no complete data is given upfront, so players must buy intelligence, judge how reliable each source is, and decide under uncertainty, much like real international business.

It is a standalone game in the BizOn ecosystem, built for International Business or Entrepreneurial Internationalization coursework. No login is required, it runs directly in the browser, and all progress is saved locally on the player's device.

### 4 companies to choose from

| Company | Product | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🧴 Mộc Nhiên | Herbal cosmetics | Strong brand story | Hard to meet international standards |
| 🥫 Phù Sa Foods | Processed food | Distinctive flavor, appeals to young buyers | High storage cost (+20% operating cost) |
| 👗 Lam Việt | Sustainable fashion | Green credentials already in place, fits Lục Đảo well | Limited production capacity |
| 💻 Mekong Digital | Management software | Very strong digital channels, high margins | Weak on data regulation |

There is no single "strongest" pick. Each company carries its own path and its own risk profile.

### 6 markets (fictional countries)

| Market | Character |
|---|---|
| 🌐 Hải Lam | Fast-growing digital market, quick entry, very high online competition |
| 🏔️ Bắc Phong | Strict standards, higher prices and prestige, heavy compliance cost, **needs certification** to unlock its full potential |
| 🌅 Kim Sa | Young customers, fast growth, trends shift constantly |
| 🏝️ Lục Đảo | Environment-first buyers, needs a sustainability record plus traceability |
| 🌸 Nhật Quang | Trust builds slowly, loyal partners, revenue grows the longer a company stays |
| ⚓ Tân Cảng | Regional gateway, a hub into several markets, high operating cost but large scale |

### 3 entry modes (pick one per market on entry)

| Mode | Capital | Character |
|---|---|---|
| 🛒 Digital platform | Low (0.5 bn) | Low commitment, fast entry, thin margins |
| 🚢 Direct export | Moderate (1.2 bn) | Moderate commitment, better control |
| 🤝 Local partner | Moderate (0.8 bn) | Borrows the partner's network, but depends on them |

## 2 · The quarterly loop, 4 steps

Each of the 6 quarters repeats the same 4 steps. Understanding this loop is understanding the whole game.

### Step 1 · 👁️ Observe & Intelligence

- The game shows **2 random market signals** each quarter (open market news plus a private analysis, or social media), each with different reliability, and some signals can be misleading.
- Players can buy more intelligence from **6 sources** (max 2 sources per quarter): industry reports, customer surveys, expert consulting, social media data (⚠️ tends to be hyped), test sales, and local-partner intel (⚠️ can be biased toward the partner's own interest). Each source has its own price, accuracy and **bias**. The core lesson: **not every source deserves the same trust**.
- Buying intel is optional; players can skip it to save cash.
- **Item Shop** (see §3) is also open at this step, for players who want a one-off edge.

### Step 2 · 🧭 Decision

Three choices on one screen, then click "🔒 Lock in this quarter":

1. **1 priority for the quarter** (required): Scout the market (intel prices −50% this quarter) · Push sales (+15% revenue, resilience −3) · Build the brand (spend 0.5 bn, reputation +6) · Upgrade capability (spend 0.8 bn, capability +8) · Strengthen resilience (resilience +8, operating cost −20%).
2. **Enter a new market** (optional, max 1 market per quarter): pick 1 of the 6 not-yet-entered markets × 1 of the 3 entry modes. An **AIBIS** score (0–100, a deterministic engine) is shown for reference, suggesting how well each mode fits that market's profile. It is illustrative only, never a substitute for the player's own judgment. A live **Lumina forecast** (see §3) also shows an estimated revenue range for the selected market and mode before the player commits.
3. **1 operating stance**: Frugal · Balanced · All-in (higher revenue but rising cost and risk).

### Step 3 · ⚡ Handle the event

An unexpected market or institutional event appears (drawn from a pool of 24, never repeating within the same run), always with 2 or more response choices, each leading to a different outcome. There is no single correct answer.

**Quarter 4 is a special case:** instead of a normal event, a **MISSION · Bắc Phong's Gate** appears: Bắc Phong's largest distributor offers to place the product in 500 stores, in exchange for a 3-year exclusive and other binding conditions. There are 5 response choices (accept everything / decline / negotiate the exclusivity away / propose a trial first / find a smaller partner). **Accepting everything only succeeds if the company has enough capability and cash**; otherwise the commitment breaks and reputation takes a heavy hit. This is a key teaching moment: a big commitment needs real internal strength to back it up.

### Step 4 · 🧾 Quarterly results

Revenue and profit for every market the company is currently active in, plus the updated cash balance, appear immediately and are logged in the **Journey log**. Click "📅 Go to quarter [n]" to continue; quarter 6 goes straight to the final results screen instead, with no "next quarter" step.

**Early loss:** running out of cash for 2 quarters in a row, or reputation dropping into crisis territory (below 15), ends the run immediately, without needing to finish all 6 quarters.

## 3 · New mechanics: Shop, Forecast, and Dynamic Markets

- **🛍️ Item Shop** (open at any time during Step 1 or 2): spend company cash on an edge. It is optional, never required to finish the game.
  - **Mystery Shopper** (0.4 bn): instantly reveals the true fit of one market, no intel source needed.
  - **Risk Insurance** (0.6 bn): cancels the negative impact of the next market event.
  - **Crisis PR Kit** (0.5 bn): reputation +8, used instantly.
  - **Lumina Data Pack** (0.4 bn): reveals next quarter's event category in advance.
  - **Bắc Phong Fast-Track Certification** (1 bn, permanent): instantly meets the Bắc Phong standard, no need to wait for the certification event.
  - **Partner Network** (0.8 bn, permanent): +10% revenue in every market entered via Local partner.
- **🔮 Lumina forecast**: as soon as a market and entry mode are selected in Step 2, Lumina shows a live estimated revenue range for that combination, narrowing as the player gathers more intel on that market.
- **📈 Dynamic markets**: competition intensity in each market reacts to the company's own performance there. A strong quarter draws in more competitors; a weak quarter eases competition off. The Journey log notes it whenever the shift is meaningful.

## 4 · Scoring formula, public from quarter 1

```
Total score = 30% Profit + 20% Reputation + 20% Capability + 15% Adaptation + 15% Sustainability
```

The highest revenue does not automatically win: overheated growth that drains cash, burns partners, or breaks regulations gets penalized. Balancing all 5 dimensions (each ≥60) unlocks the top title: **🏆 A Vietnamese Brand Sets Sail**.

## 5 · The end screen

- 🎖️ A badge plus a total score out of 100, with a detailed chart across the 5 criteria.
- 🎓 **Certificate of completion**: shown only for a full 6-quarter run (not an early loss). Players can download a signed, printable PNG certificate with their company name and final score.
- 👣 **Decision footprint**: a list of the choices that left a long-term mark on the run (e.g. signing an exclusive, registering trademark protection early).
- 📡 **Submit results to the instructor** (optional): students enter a class code and their name, and results go straight into the system, visible on the instructor dashboard with no manual file collection needed.
- 🎓 **4 debrief questions** for class discussion (which source felt most trustworthy and why, why that entry mode was chosen, which decision left the longest-lasting effect, why two teams starting with the same resources can end up in very different places).
- 📋 A direct link to the post-session survey (International Business track).

## 6 · For instructors

1. Share the play link with the class, no account needed: `https://thuyhuongctu.github.io/BizOn/brand-passport.html`
2. Agree on a **class code** (e.g. `IB-2026-UMK`) for students to enter when submitting results.
3. Watch the whole class live on the **instructor dashboard** (`giang-vien.html`, "🛂 Brand Passport" section): see each team's score, export to CSV for grading.
4. **Detailed grading criteria** (formula, rubric, sample gradebook): see `docs/cham-diem-ho-chieu-thuong-hieu.md` (Vietnamese) or §4.2 of `docs/chuan-dau-ra-va-rubric.md`. Ask the BizOn team for an English rubric summary if needed for accreditation paperwork.
5. For an international class: the game is **fully bilingual** (VI/EN). Students just click the language toggle in the top-left "⋯" menu; the guide videos have their own English-captioned version too.
6. Suggested session length: a full 6-quarter run takes about 20–30 minutes of real play; the detailed guide video runs about 95 seconds, enough to open the class with instead of a live walkthrough.

## 7 · For students

- There is no single "correct" strategy. Read each company's and market's description carefully before choosing.
- Buy intel wisely: a more expensive source is not always more trustworthy. Watch for the ⚠️ warnings on some sources.
- Read the quarter-4 MISSION's terms carefully before accepting everything; it needs both enough capability **and** enough cash to succeed.
- After finishing, read back your own "Decision footprint" before answering the debrief questions; that is the data to reason from.
- Replay as many times as you like (the "🔄 Play again" button) to compare different strategies on the same seed or a new one.
