# BizOn 90-day commercial pilot plan

## Goal

Turn BizOn from a strong working prototype into an evidence-backed, licensable educational product. The plan protects the current multi-game identity and does not claim that intellectual-property registration has been completed.

## Success criteria by day 90

- at least 2 real course pilots
- at least 100 participating students
- at least 70% complete the assigned simulation
- average learner satisfaction at least 4.0/5
- usable pre/post learning dataset with a documented instrument
- at least 3 institutional conversations
- at least 1 paid pilot or signed letter of intent
- instructor can export a class-level result file
- Google Play closed-test package ready or in testing
- complete evidence folder for copyright/trademark consultation

Targets are decision thresholds, not current achievements.

## Phase 1 — Product truth and readiness (days 1–14)

Deliverables:

- position BizOn as one ecosystem containing many games
- align README, manifest, Arcade language and store listing
- publish an accurate Vietnamese–English privacy policy
- remove unsupported claims and distinguish simulation data from empirical data
- define a stable pilot version and freeze feature additions during classroom testing
- create a bug and feedback intake form
- prepare instructor onboarding and a 45-minute lesson flow

Exit gate:

- a new instructor can start a class without direct help from the founders
- no critical broken paths on phone, tablet and desktop

## Phase 2 — Evidence pilot (days 15–45)

Recommended design:

- pre-test before exposure
- assigned BizOn activity
- post-test using matched learning outcomes
- short usability and engagement questionnaire
- instructor observation log
- anonymous class code; do not collect unnecessary personal data

Minimum measures:

- completion rate
- time to first meaningful decision
- number of rounds completed
- decision-quality rubric
- pre/post knowledge score
- perceived usefulness and ease of use
- intention to use again
- qualitative failure points

Do not report causal learning effects unless the design supports the claim. A one-group pre/post pilot is product evidence, not a randomized trial.

## Phase 3 — Paid pilot and licensing test (days 46–75)

Test price bands:

| Offer | Pilot price |
|---|---:|
| One class / one semester | VND 5–12 million |
| Faculty or department / one year | VND 30–70 million |
| University / one year | VND 80–200 million |
| Custom content or co-branding | VND 80–250 million per project |
| Instructor training workshop | VND 5–15 million per session |

Start with one paid pilot. Do not discount to zero after a partner has accepted the educational value; offer a limited pilot scope instead.

Commercial documents:

- pilot proposal
- scope and acceptance criteria
- non-exclusive license
- support boundaries
- data-processing statement
- invoice/payment terms
- feedback and testimonial permission as separate opt-ins

## Phase 4 — Play and scale gate (days 76–90)

- build signed Android App Bundle
- complete closed testing
- prepare screenshots and feature graphic
- fix high-severity pre-launch issues
- document release ownership and signing-key custody
- decide whether backend/multi-class synchronization is justified by pilot demand

## Valuation working note

Internal planning range at the prototype stage: VND 300–700 million, with VND 500 million as a working midpoint. This is not a certified valuation and must not be presented as guaranteed market value.

A VND 1.5–3 billion pre-seed narrative should only be used after evidence such as paid pilots, letters of intent, repeat usage, learning outcomes, backend readiness and clearer IP ownership is available.

## Roles

- Founder & Creative Lead: product scope, partner discovery, pilot coordination, brand
- Co-founder & Academic Advisor: learning outcomes, teaching validity, institutional review
- Technical contributor: release stability, Android build, export and privacy checks
- Pilot instructor: class delivery, observation and feedback

Names and ownership percentages must be governed by a separate signed agreement; this roadmap does not allocate equity or IP.
