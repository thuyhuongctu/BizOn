## English

### Short description

A multi-game ecosystem for business, entrepreneurship and global decisions.

### Full description

**One app, many games — learn business by making decisions yourself.**

BizOn Bật Nghiệp is an educational business-simulation ecosystem for university learners, instructors and aspiring entrepreneurs.

In **BizOn Vietnam**, you run a virtual company through six rounds across a map of Vietnam. Set price, marketing, R&D, production, workforce, funding and payment terms, then observe how the market, cash flow, profit and AI rivals respond.

In **BizOn GO GlObal**, choose an international market and an entry mode: export, licensing, joint venture or greenfield investment. IE Lab lets learners explore simulated internationalization–performance scenarios. IE Lab values are illustrative teaching simulations, not real company statistics.

**BizOn Arcade** adds multiple knowledge, strategy and reflex games, including Clay Factory Frenzy, Entrepreneurship Quiz, Market Price Guess, Clay Sort, Head-to-Head Arena and Catch the Golden Capital.

Key features:

- multiple games in one ecosystem
- solo play with AI rivals
- Vietnam and GO GlObal modes
- financial reports and decision feedback
- learning achievements and challenges
- Vietnamese–English and light–dark interfaces
- installable with partial offline support
- no ads or in-app purchases in the current version

BizOn is designed to supplement instructor-led learning, not replace instruction.
