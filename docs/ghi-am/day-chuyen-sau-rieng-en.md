# Recording script – Durian Packing Line (English)

Every piece of text shown on the page: **147 files**. **34 already recorded** (marked ✅ – no need to record again), **113 new** (marked 🆕).

## How to record
- Each item is **one separate file**, named exactly as on its «File» line (.mp3, .m4a or .wav all fine).
- Natural pace, half a second of silence before and after.
- «°C» is read “degrees Celsius”; «−18 °C» “minus eighteen degrees”; «%» “per cent”.
- Dates such as «1 Jun 2024» are read “the first of June twenty twenty-four”.
- «TCVN 10739:2015» is read “T-C-V-N ten seven three nine, twenty fifteen”; «HACCP» “hassup”; «≥» “or more”.
- Do not read the «» marks or the symbols ▶ 💡 📚. Vietnamese names (Bactrocera correcta, Tuổi Trẻ…) as written.
- Numbers chosen by the player (the score, the % or hours on the sliders) cannot be pre-recorded; those few words stay with the browser voice.
- Send back: zip the folder, or send the files into this chat.

## 4. The eleven stations – introduction

**✅ File: `en/tram-01-cat.mp3`** – already recorded

> Harvest. Cut with the stalk on; never let fruit drop to the ground (bruising, fungal infection). Lay fruit on padding, not in a heap in the sun.

**✅ File: `en/tram-02-tiepNhan.mp3`** – already recorded

> Intake & area code. Each lot records the planting-area code and the grower. Official exports must trace back to a registered planting area.

**✅ File: `en/tram-03-phanLoai.mp3`** – already recorded

> Grading & weighing. Tap and listen, check spines and stalk; reject cracked, borer-damaged or misshapen fruit; weigh and grade to the order. TCVN 10739:2015 grades by full locules: Extra 4 or more, Grade I 3, Grade II 2.

**✅ File: `en/tram-04-lamSach.mp3`** – already recorded

> Cleaning & treatment. Brush soil out from between the spines, air-blow or rinse; check and treat mealybugs and fruit rot. The 2022 protocol lists 6 quarantine pests: the fruit fly Bactrocera correcta and 5 mealybug species; any soil, leaves or live pest blocks the whole lot.

**✅ File: `en/tram-05-kiemMau.mp3`** – already recorded

> Lab sampling. Send samples to a mutually approved lab: pesticide residues, cadmium (required from 1 Jun 2024), auramine O (added from 10 Jan 2025). The lot is closed only once the test report is in.

**✅ File: `en/tram-06-tachMui.mp3`** – already recorded

> Opening & de-segmenting. Open fruit in a clean area with gloves and masks; keep whole segments, reject hard or mushy ones. From here it is exposed food and easily contaminated.

**✅ File: `en/tram-07-hutChan.mp3`** – already recorded

> Vacuum packing. Lay segments in trays or bags, vacuum and seal. Less air means less oxidation and the aroma stays in.

**✅ File: `en/tram-08-capDong.mp3`** – already recorded

> Blast freezing. Chill very fast so ice crystals stay small and thawed pulp keeps its texture; slow freezing makes it mushy. Malaysia’s model SOP freezes whole fruit at −80 to −110 °C for at least 1 hour, after a 500 ppm chlorine wash.

**✅ File: `en/tram-09-dongThung.mp3`** – already recorded

> Labelling & boxing. Label with the planting-area code, packhouse code and product name; pack into cartons to the order spec. Under the protocol, boxes carry both codes; goods from unregistered sources are refused.

**✅ File: `en/tram-10-kho.mp3`** – already recorded

> Cold store & reefer. Into the cold store, then a reefer container. Fresh fruit stays cool; frozen stays deep-frozen. Set the temperature with the slider below.

**✅ File: `en/tram-11-kiemDich.mp3`** – already recorded

> Quarantine & customs. Plant-quarantine officers inspect the lot and issue the certificate; the file must match the area code, packhouse code and lab report. Protocol: 2% of each lot sampled in the first 2 years, 1% after if clean.

## 6. Sourced facts («📚 Sourced facts» box)

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-thaiDry.mp3`**

> Thailand raised the export dry-matter standard from 32% to 35% and checks every lot. Source: Ministry of Industry and Trade (2023). Báo cáo thị trường rau quả tháng 12/2023 [Fruit and vegetable market report, Dec 2023].

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-loss.mp3`**

> Whole-chain loss 15–40%, of which 15–20% is natural moisture loss; rejected fruit sells 50–80% cheaper. Source: Master's thesis (2014). Giải pháp hoàn thiện chuỗi cung ứng sầu riêng tại huyện Cai Lậy, Tiền Giang [Improving the durian supply chain in Cai Lậy].

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-codes.mp3`**

> Planting areas and packing facilities must be registered and approved by both sides; boxes carry both codes. Goods from unregistered sources are refused. Source: Protocol on phytosanitary requirements for fresh durian exported from Viet Nam to China (MARD–GACC, 2022), Art. 2–9 and Annex.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-grades.mp3`**

> Extra grade ≥4 full locules, Grade I ≥3, Grade II ≥2; 10% quality tolerance. Source: TCVN 10739:2015 – Fresh durian (Vietnamese national standard).

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-pests.mp3`**

> 6 quarantine pests, including the fruit fly Bactrocera correcta and 5 mealybug species. Source: Protocol on phytosanitary requirements for fresh durian exported from Viet Nam to China (MARD–GACC, 2022), Art. 2–9 and Annex.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-cdAuramine.mp3`**

> 1/6/2024 · 10/1/2025 · From 1 Jun 2024 every Vietnamese durian lot must be tested for cadmium at a mutually approved lab; from 10 Jan 2025 an auramine O certificate is also required, and China re-checks 100% of lots at the border. Source: Dân Việt (24 May 2025); Tuổi Trẻ (6 Feb 2025). GACC requirements for cadmium and auramine O testing of imported durian.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-cadmium.mp3`**

> From May 2023 to Jan 2024, China Customs flagged 30 durian lots from 18 firms for cadmium above the limit. Source: Tuổi Trẻ (23 Mar 2024). Why 30 durian lots exported to China were contaminated with cadmium (30 lots flagged from May 2023 to Jan 2024); CESTI (2024), agro.gov.vn news of 8 Apr 2024 (18 firms).

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-frozenSigned.mp3`**

> 19/8/2024 · MARD and GACC signed the frozen-durian protocol (whole fruit, purée, pulp). Packing houses need HACCP, traceability, and adequate freezing and cold-storage capacity. Source: VTV/VGP (19 Aug 2024). Frozen durian officially licensed for China (MARD–GACC protocol signed).

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-frozenMY.mp3`**

> Whole-fruit freezing at −80 to −110 °C for at least 1 hour after a 500 ppm chlorine wash; in a trial 50% of fruit cracked because of low humidity. Source: MARDI (2023). The SOP for exporting frozen whole durian fruit.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-frozenStandard.mp3`**

> The international Codex (FAO/WHO) standard for quick-frozen foods: store and distribute at −18°C or below. Source: Codex Alimentarius – FAO/WHO, CXC 8-1976 (Code of Practice for the Processing and Handling of Quick Frozen Foods): store and distribute quick-frozen food at −18°C or below.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-freshStorage.mp3`**

> A Thai semi-commercial trial: mature fresh durian stored at about 13.5°C lasted up to 15 days; younger fruit showed chilling injury at that temperature. Source: Boonyaritthongchai, P. et al. Storage of Durians on a Semi-Commercial Scale. Agriculture and Natural Resources (Thailand), FAO AGRIS index.

**🆕 File: `en/du-kien-sampling.mp3`**

Source: Protocol on phytosanitary requirements for fresh durian exported from Viet Nam to China (MARD–GACC, 2022), Art. 2–9 and Annex.
