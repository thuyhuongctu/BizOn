# IP status and evidence checklist

Status date: 27 July 2026.

## Public wording

Use:

> © 2026 Do Thuy Huong and Phan Anh Tu. All rights reserved. Registration dossiers are being prepared.

Do not use:

- “registered intellectual property”
- “registered trademark”
- “copyright certificate issued”
- “patented”
- any registration number until it has been verified against the issued document

The Zenodo DOI, Git history, `CITATION.cff`, proprietary license and dated releases document provenance. They are not substitutes for an issued registration certificate.

## Evidence to preserve

- source snapshot for each stable version
- Git commit and release history
- Zenodo deposit and DOI metadata
- `CITATION.cff`
- dated design files and prompt/provenance notes for visual assets
- original audio, lyrics and production records
- simulation-model specification
- instructor guide and dated pilot materials
- contributor list and contribution records
- contracts or written permissions for third-party assets
- evidence of first public use of the BizOn and Lumina names

## Ownership questions to resolve before investment or exclusive licensing

- authors and percentage ownership
- relationship between personal work and institutional duties/resources
- ownership of commissioned or AI-assisted visual/audio assets
- right to use names, photographs and likenesses
- rights granted to pilots, sponsors and collaborators
- who may sign licenses and receive revenue
- what belongs in the public BizOn repository versus the private internal repository

## Repository rule

Keep non-sensitive public evidence in this repository. Keep identity documents, draft registration forms, signatures, private contracts, financial details, signing keys and legal correspondence in the private internal repository or another access-controlled location.

This checklist is operational project documentation, not legal advice.
