Bật Nghiệp game: grading scale

Compiled by Đỗ Thùy Hương & Phan Anh Tú

Scored out of 10. All points come from game results and the contribution log; there is no report or presentation. Team scores are shared; only B5 is graded individually.

1. Before you play

  • Each member signs in on their own device with the same Class code and Team name, and selects their role (CEO, CFO, CMO, COO or SEC).
  • Without a Class code the game runs in practice mode and is not graded.
  • Each team plays 6 rounds. In every round the CEO presses Commit to lock in the decisions.

4. How contributions (B5) are counted

On the Decisions screen, use the «📋 Contribution log» panel under the Team meeting. Only entries sent before the CEO presses Commit count.

Role Each round Counts as «adopted» when
CFOSet R&D and payment terms → «Send proposal to the CEO»The committed decision is within 10% of your proposal
CMOSet price and marketing → «Send proposal to the CEO»Same as above
COOSet production, workforce and training → «Send proposal to the CEO»Same as above
SECWrite the meeting minutes: the event, each role's view, the agreed decision → «Send minutes»The minutes are at least 30 characters long
CEOReview the team's proposals, press «Apply», then CommitThe committed round uses at least one proposal from the CFO, CMO or COO

Each What-If run also counts as participation in that round. Sending several times in one round earns no extra points; the team uses the latest version.
